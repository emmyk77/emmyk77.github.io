Wealthsimple marketing analytics

A seven-page Power BI report follows campaign spending from signup to paying customer using fictional training data.

Business question

What does it cost to acquire a paying customer when campaign records are compared on the same basis?

My approach

I checked 2,000 campaign records for missing costs, negative values and inconsistent funnel counts. I used the 1,671 eligible records to compare spend and customer counts, then built report pages for acquisition cost, conversion, revenue and budget review.

What I found

The eligible records contained CAD 426,714.30 in marketing costs and 106 recorded paying customers. That gives an acquisition cost of CAD 4,025.61 per recorded paying customer. The signup-to-paying rate was 0.77%.

These figures describe a training scenario. Customer identifiers would be needed to establish how many unique people were acquired.
