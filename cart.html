E-commerce cart abandonment

An Excel study of 10,000 cart events shows where abandoned item value was concentrated and what to test next.

Business question

Which carts account for the largest share of abandoned item value?

My approach

I checked the cart records, built formula-linked summaries in Excel and compared abandonment by device, price band, category and time. Each record contains one item in one session.

What I found

Of 10,000 cart events, 4,988 were flagged as abandoned. Items priced above $300 accounted for 64.48% of abandoned item value. That makes them a sensible group for a controlled recovery experiment.

Item price is a proxy for cart value. The dataset has no confirmed payment, shipping fee or checkout-step records, so it cannot establish lost sales or explain why a shopper left.
