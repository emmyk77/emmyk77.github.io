Excel   /   Campaign reporting

GetShopToday campaign performance

An Excel dashboard compares campaign spend, clicks, paid customers and revenue across marketing channels.

Business question

Do the campaigns that bring in the most customers also generate the most revenue?

My approach

I summarised campaign results in PivotTables and added dashboard slicers for product category, customer location and referral source. This lets a reader explore spending, traffic and sales within the same view.

What I found

Influencer campaigns led with 60 paid customers, followed by pay-per-click campaigns with 54. Pay-per-click campaigns recorded the highest displayed revenue at about $12,000.

The dashboard’s ROI and acquisition-cost fields require a formula review before they can support profitability decisions.
